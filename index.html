Unlike crowded locations, PeterN's Beach Resort is designed as a singular, grand beach house. When you book, you aren't just getting a room; you are securing an entire coastal sanctuary for your family or group.

The resort features high vaulted ceilings, panoramic ocean windows, and a seamless indoor-outdoor flow that lets the sea breeze travel through the expansive living areas.

The Al Fresco
Chef's Kitchen

Dining at PeterN's is an open-air experience. The fully equipped outdoor kitchen features a wood-fired pizza oven, a massive grill station, and a bespoke teak dining table that seats 14.

Hire our private chef for a local seafood feast, or grill your own catch of the day while listening to the waves crash just yards away.

Wedding Receptions

Say "I do" with the ocean as your backdrop. The resort's manicured beachfront lawn transforms into a magical reception area capable of hosting up to 80 guests.
